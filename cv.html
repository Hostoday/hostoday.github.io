Graduate Research Student

ISNLP Lab, Gachon University · Advisor: Prof. Sangwoo Kang

KLOD — Locality-Preserving Sequential Knowledge Editing

EMNLP 2026 Findings

  • Identified unintended drift in non-target output distributions under cross-entropy editing; designed bounded target logit-odds optimization with distribution-preserving KL constraints.
  • Led implementation, baselines, experimental design, and ablations across two model backbones and two datasets; evaluated sequential editing up to 150,000 edits.
  • On LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct / CounterFact, improved locality from 1.87 to 44.75 while retaining 99.70 reliability; on zsRE, achieved 86.47 locality and 99.89 reliability.

PLFT-KE — Target-Prior-Aware Fine-Tuning for Knowledge Editing

Under review

  • Studied forgetting using controlled synthetic biographies with 50K pre-training and 50K fine-tuning individuals; found target-token prior more informative than general output-token confidence in this setting.
  • Developed a two-stage probability-lift initialization: optimize a latent shift with the model frozen, convert it into a rank-1 weight update, then consolidate using standard fine-tuning.
  • On LLaMA-3.2-3B / CounterFact, improved FT-M score from 58.43 to 72.41 and specificity from 33.55 to 59.40; observed weaker and heterogeneous gains in long sequential editing.

Beyond Norm Growth — Geometric Analysis of Hidden-State Drift

Under review

  • Decomposed base-relative hidden-state displacement into parallel and orthogonal components to distinguish representation drift from scalar norm growth.
  • Observed an approximately 98.7–99.3% checkpoint-median orthogonal displacement-energy share at the measured boundary; tested orthogonal interventions against same-final-norm parallel controls.
  • Across 34 eligible endpoint states, 25% orthogonal reduction yielded mean changes of +0.729 percentage points in locality and +0.333 percentage points in teacher-forced efficacy, with heterogeneous effects.

Ongoing Research — Early-Stage Forgetting and Layer-Wise Transformations

  • Investigating early fine-tuning forgetting and layer-wise rotational/scaling changes to connect representation subspaces, output distributions, and factual recall.
