KLOD: Locality-Preserving Knowledge Editing via Non-Target Distribution Preservation

Hojun Jeong*, Gyunyeon Kim*, and Sangwoo Kang

Under review at ACL Rolling Review, May 2026 cycle · *Equal contribution

Introduces a bounded, distribution-preserving objective for fine-tuning-based knowledge editing. KLOD combines a one-sided target logit-odds objective with KL preservation of the renormalized non-target distribution at target positions and full next-token distribution preservation at prefix positions. Across 3,000 sequential edits on CounterFact and ZsRE, KLOD substantially improves locality while maintaining high edit reliability.

ARR · May 2026

Target-Token Prior as a Predictor of Forgetting in Fine-Tuning-Based Knowledge Editing

Hojun Jeong*, Gyunyeon Kim*, and Sangwoo Kang

Under review at ACL Rolling Review, May 2026 cycle · *Equal contribution

Identifies first-token target prior as a signal associated with forgetting in fine-tuning-based knowledge editing and proposes Probability-Lift Fine-Tuning for Knowledge Editing (PLFT-KE). PLFT-KE lifts low-prior targets in activation space, converts the resulting latent shift into a rank-1 parameter initialization, and then performs fine-tuning consolidation. Experiments on CounterFact and ZsRE show improved generalization and specificity over fine-tuning-based baselines while preserving edit efficacy.
