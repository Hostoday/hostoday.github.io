Hojun Jeong

M.S. Student in Artificial Intelligence at Gachon University

I am a graduate researcher in the NLP / AI Lab at Gachon University, advised by Prof. Sangwoo Kang.

My research studies how factual knowledge changes during post-training. I am particularly interested in knowledge editing, catastrophic forgetting, sequential updates, and the representation dynamics that connect internal model changes to output distributions.

My current work develops locality-preserving objectives for fine-tuning-based knowledge editing and investigates why some target facts remain stable while others degrade rapidly. Earlier, I worked on controllable dialogue summarization using structured prompting and verify-refine pipelines.

  • Knowledge editing and continual updates: preserving edit reliability and locality across sequences of model updates.
  • Forgetting diagnostics: identifying token-level and optimization-level signals associated with knowledge degradation.
  • Mechanistic analysis: relating layer-wise transformations, predictive distributions, and computational pathways.

Research overview

More detail →

01

Sequential knowledge editing

Fine-tuning can insert a target fact while altering unrelated predictions, and these side effects can accumulate across sequential edits. My KLOD project studies whether preserving the non-target token distribution provides a more reliable locality constraint.

02

Predicting and mitigating forgetting

I investigate target-token prior probability as a diagnostic signal for post-edit degradation. This line of work also examines why interventions that appear effective for a single edit can fail under repeated updates.

03

Layer-wise representation dynamics

My ongoing work analyzes early-stage forgetting from rotational and scaling perspectives, with the goal of connecting representation-space transformations to predictive behavior and knowledge expression.
