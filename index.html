NLP · Large Language Models

Hojun Jeong

M.S. Student in Artificial Intelligence at Gachon University

I am a graduate researcher in the NLP / AI Lab at Gachon University, advised by Prof. Sangwoo Kang.

I study how knowledge changes during post-training, with a particular focus on knowledge editing, catastrophic forgetting, and the representation dynamics that emerge under sequential updates.

  • Knowledge editing and continual updates: preserving locality and reliability across sequences of model edits.
  • Forgetting diagnostics: identifying token-level and optimization-level signals associated with knowledge degradation.
  • Mechanistic analysis: connecting layer-wise transformations, predictive distributions, and computational pathways.
